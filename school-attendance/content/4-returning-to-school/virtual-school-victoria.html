Virtual School Victoria

Virtual School Victoria is a flexible and online school for Foundation to Year 12 students in Victoria. VSV is for students with diverse needs and circumstances that prevent them from accessing mainstream schooling. At VSV, students learn through online courses, real-time online classes and face-to-face communication.

Enrolment

Who is eligible?

Students may be eligible to enrol at VSV if they:

  1. have Victorian residency
  2. enrol for a minimum of two consecutive school terms or six consecutive months within a single school year
  3. are between the minimum age of 4 years and 9 months and under the age of 21 years as of 1 January of that school year.

Learn more about VSV’s requirements for all students.

What are the enrolment requirements? (focus on mental health requirements)

Besides meeting basic eligibility criteria for all students, depending on the reasons students have for enrolling at VSV, there are additional enrolment requirements.

School-based enrolment

Students who maintain their current enrolment in a mainstream school, and enrol at VSV to complete subjects that are either not offered at their school or not available due to timetabling clashes or other issues. See VSV’s requirements for school-based enrolments.

Direct enrolment

Students whose circumstances prevent them from accessing mainstream schooling, and fall under one of the six enrolment categories, may enrol directly with VSV.

  • Medical: Physical Health or Mental Health
  • Travel
  • Sports/Performance
  • Distance
  • Previous Home Schooler
  • Young Adult

In general, parents must provide documented evidence as part of their child’s direct enrolment application.

For example, students enrolling in the Medical: Mental Health category must have a diagnosed mental health or social emotional difficulty, meaning it is not possible for them to attend mainstream school for an extended period of time.

VSV recognises that an important prerequisite for successful engagement with education is the establishment of effective ongoing treatment and support for mental health and social emotional difficulties.

Students must demonstrate they are receiving ongoing professional treatment and/or support for their condition/s. The referring practitioner must commit to coordination of the care plan for this student. Please note that these supports must be in place prior to submitting an application to VSV

Learn more about VSV’s direct enrolment requirements.

Can my child do part-time VSV and part-time mainstream school?

Yes. If your child is currently enrolled at a Victorian Government or Non-Government School mainstream school, they can enrol at VSV to complete subjects that are either not offered at their school or not available due to timetabling clashes or other issues.

Year 7 – 10 school-based students can study a maximum of one subject/unit with VSV each semester. Year 11 and 12 school-based students can study a maximum of two subjects with VSV each semester.

Additional requirements apply. See VSV’s Students in other schools.

Can my child to do all of their schooling at VSV?

Yes. VSV offers one of the largest ranges of subjects of any school in Victoria, from Foundation to Year 12.

VSV aims to be flexible to meet the circumstances of each of their students. Teachers advise you on how to modify and adapt the course to meet the learning needs of your child.

See a full list of subjects by year level.

Can my child do Victorian Certificate of Education (VCE) at VSV?

Yes. In Years F – 10, VSV’s learning programs are based on the Victorian Curriculum. In Years 11 – 12 VSV follows the curriculum set by the Victorian Curriculum and Assessment Authority (VCAA) in our wide range of Victorian Certificate of Education (VCE) subjects.

Find out about subjects offered by VSV in Year 11 and Year 12.

What does it cost? What resources are needed at home?

There are no enrolment fees for students who are eligible to directly enrol with VSV.

Enrolment costs for school-based students vary. See VSV’s Students in other schools.

At home, all VSV students must have regular and reliable access to a computer/laptop and the internet, to access and participate in the online courses, communicate and submit their work.

View the full list of minimum technical specifications and any textbooks/resources that need to be purchased.

There may be additional technological and other requirements for individual subjects. See VSV’s subject information pages.

How do I apply? Are there cut-off dates for enrolment?

Students who are eligible can apply in two ways.

For students in other schools, your child’s school must complete and submit the Students in Schools online application form. See Students in Schools enrolment.

For direct enrolments, parents will need to complete an online Enrolment Application Form and obtain the necessary supporting documentation for their child’s enrolment category. You can submit these to VSV’s Enrolment Office by email, post, or in-person at their Thornbury address:

315 Clarendon Street, Thornbury, VIC 3071

There are cut-off dates for enrolment. There are two intakes each year, one for each semester of the school year.

VSV operates on the same term dates as all other Victorian Government Schools. Semester 1 includes Term 1 and Term 2. Semester 2 includes Term 3 and Term 4.

See the VSV website for enrolment dates.

How quickly can my child be enrolled?

It depends. After an application is submitted during the intake period, VSV will:

  1. review the application to determine eligibility
  2. confirm an appropriate learning program
  3. provide online access.

Once these are completed, an enrolment confirmation and login details to access VSV Online (a learning management system) will be emailed to you.

VSV may need more time to confirm your child’s enrolment if further evidence is required, or to develop a suitable learning program for your child.
